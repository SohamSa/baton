# Observable boundary

The inference path and the policy see `OBS_KEYS` on accelerator rows plus runtime logs, checkpoints, and collector freshness. They do not receive future fault times, hidden resistance, seed identifiers as features, or repair outcomes.

`public_view` removes the evaluator block, raw observation log used for grading, and ledger errors. Presentation mode also removes metrics and comparison numbers.

`GET /api/v1/runs/{id}/truth` requires the administrator role and returns a payload labeled evaluator-only latent truth. The device and incident pages do not call it.

A leakage test changes hidden fields while holding the observed series fixed and checks that the operational assessment stays the same.
